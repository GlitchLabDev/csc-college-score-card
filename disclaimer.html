The short version: My University View is a research tool. The numbers come from historical government datasets, real prices are usually lower than sticker prices, and every important detail should be verified directly with the college before you act on it.

College data is historical, not live

Tuition, acceptance rates, enrollment, test scores, campus settings, and fields of study come primarily from the US Department of Education's College Scorecard — official data that is nonetheless one to three years behind the present by the time it is published. Colleges change prices, policies, and programs every year. Nothing in the App is a quote, offer, or guarantee of any cost or admission outcome.

Sticker price is not real price

Published tuition rarely reflects what families actually pay. Most colleges award institutional grants and discounts — private colleges discount first-year tuition by roughly half on average, per NACUBO research. Use the App's numbers to compare schools, then get a real cost from each college's net-price calculator and financial-aid office.

Athletics information

Division labels, conferences, coaches, and records are compiled from public sources and change frequently — coaching changes and conference realignment happen every season. Recruiting rules are governed by the NCAA, NAIA, and each institution; always confirm current information with the school's athletic department and the relevant governing body.
