Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Ohio?
48 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Ohio?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Cuyahoga Community College District in Cleveland is lowest at $5,864 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Ohio?
The middle of the range is about $8,667 a year after aid, from $5,864 to $18,134. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
