Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Maryland?
21 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Maryland?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Aspen Beauty Academy of Laurel in Laurel is lowest at $9,774 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Maryland?
The middle of the range is about $20,277 a year after aid, from $9,774 to $30,358. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
