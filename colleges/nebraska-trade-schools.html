How many trade and technical schools are there in Nebraska?
6 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Nebraska?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Joseph's College Cosmetology in Lincoln is lowest at $10,888 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Nebraska?
The middle of the range is about $14,141 a year after aid, from $10,888 to $17,606. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
