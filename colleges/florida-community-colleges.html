Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Florida?
40 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the community colleges in Florida?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Hillsborough Community College in Tampa is lowest at $4,264 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do community colleges cost in Florida?
The middle of the range is about $22,977 a year after aid, from $4,264 to $65,839. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
