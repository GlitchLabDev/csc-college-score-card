How many trade and technical schools are there in Rhode Island?

What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Rhode Island?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — New England Tractor Trailer Training School of Rhode Island in Pawtucket is lowest at $14,672 a year. What do trade and technical schools cost in Rhode Island?
The middle of the range is about $19,145 a year after aid, from $14,672 to $38,491. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
