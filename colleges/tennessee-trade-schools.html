Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Tennessee?
63 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Tennessee?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Tennessee College of Applied Technology-Crump in Crump is lowest at $1,126 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Tennessee?
The middle of the range is about $10,175 a year after aid, from $1,126 to $29,609. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
