Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in New York?
110 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in New York?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Career School of NY in Staten Island is lowest at $1,929 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in New York?
The middle of the range is about $17,831 a year after aid, from $1,929 to $51,258. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
