Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Arkansas?
25 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Arkansas?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Arkansas State University Three Rivers in Malvern is lowest at $3,110 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Arkansas?
The middle of the range is about $6,776 a year after aid, from $3,110 to $13,070. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
