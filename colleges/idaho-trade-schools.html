Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Idaho?
18 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Idaho?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Evans Hairstyling College-Rexburg in Rexburg is lowest at $8,863 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Idaho?
The middle of the range is about $18,232 a year after aid, from $8,863 to $29,912. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
