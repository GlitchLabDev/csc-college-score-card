Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Arkansas?
26 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Arkansas?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Northwest Technical Institute in Springdale is lowest at $6,179 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Arkansas?
The middle of the range is about $13,017 a year after aid, from $6,179 to $26,187. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
