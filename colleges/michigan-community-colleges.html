Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Michigan?
26 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Michigan?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — West Shore Community College in Scottville is lowest at $1,313 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Michigan?
The middle of the range is about $6,729 a year after aid, from $1,313 to $9,849. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
