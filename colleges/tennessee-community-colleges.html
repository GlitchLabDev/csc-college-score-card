Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Tennessee?
24 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Tennessee?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Roane State Community College in Harriman is lowest at $3,393 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Tennessee?
The middle of the range is about $6,471 a year after aid, from $3,393 to $10,131. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
