Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Nevada?
6 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the community colleges in Nevada?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Northwest Career College in Las Vegas is lowest at $13,211 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do community colleges cost in Nevada?
The middle of the range is about $30,679 a year after aid, from $13,211 to $48,103. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
