Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Colorado?
23 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Colorado?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Emily Griffith Technical College in Denver is lowest at $5,249 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Colorado?
The middle of the range is about $18,729 a year after aid, from $5,249 to $44,919. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
