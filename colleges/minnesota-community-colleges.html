Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Minnesota?
29 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Minnesota?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Red Lake Nation College in Red Lake is lowest at $6,557 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Minnesota?
The middle of the range is about $11,997 a year after aid, from $6,557 to $16,748. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
