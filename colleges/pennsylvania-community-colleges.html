Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Pennsylvania?
59 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Pennsylvania?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Butler County Community College in Butler is lowest at $6,022 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Pennsylvania?
The middle of the range is about $8,921 a year after aid, from $6,022 to $15,397. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
