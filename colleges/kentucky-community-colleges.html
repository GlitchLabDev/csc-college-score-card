Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Kentucky?
21 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Kentucky?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Southeast Kentucky Community & Technical College in Cumberland is lowest at $3,259 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Kentucky?
The middle of the range is about $5,023 a year after aid, from $3,259 to $6,764. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
