Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Nevada?
16 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Nevada?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Avalon Institute-Las Vegas in Las Vegas is lowest at $13,362 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Nevada?
The middle of the range is about $21,331 a year after aid, from $13,362 to $35,726. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
