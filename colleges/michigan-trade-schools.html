Common questions

How many trade and technical schools are there in Michigan?
70 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the trade and technical schools in Michigan?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Bayshire Academy of Beauty Craft Inc in Bay City is lowest at $5,274 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do trade and technical schools cost in Michigan?
The middle of the range is about $17,648 a year after aid, from $5,274 to $39,568. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
