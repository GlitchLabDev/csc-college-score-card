Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Virginia?
35 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Virginia?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — J Sargeant Reynolds Community College in Richmond is lowest at $3,492 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Virginia?
The middle of the range is about $6,272 a year after aid, from $3,492 to $12,062. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
