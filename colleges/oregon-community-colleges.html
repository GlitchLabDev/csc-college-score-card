Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Oregon?
19 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Oregon?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Treasure Valley Community College in Ontario is lowest at $3,248 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Oregon?
The middle of the range is about $8,807 a year after aid, from $3,248 to $13,445. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
