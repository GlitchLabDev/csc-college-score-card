Common questions

How many community colleges are there in Wisconsin?
16 — every one of them is listed above, from US Department of Education data.
What is the cheapest of the public community colleges in Wisconsin?
By average net price — what students actually pay after grants and scholarships — Nicolet Area Technical College in Rhinelander is lowest at $6,936 a year. That is an average across all students, not a quote: your own price depends on your family finances and your record.
What do public community colleges cost in Wisconsin?
The middle of the range is about $10,107 a year after aid, from $6,936 to $11,589. Published tuition is higher — the figures above are what students paid.
