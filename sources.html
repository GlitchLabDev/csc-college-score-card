Data Sources

Glitch Labs · Updated July 2026

My University View is built on official, primarily governmental data. These are the sources powering the App. Figures are historical by nature — always verify details with each institution (see our Disclaimer).

College Scorecard — US Department of Education

Our primary source: the list of accredited colleges and their tuition, acceptance rates, enrollment, test scores, campus settings, and fields of study.

Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS) — National Center for Education Statistics (NCES)

The federal survey system underlying College Scorecard's institution-level data.

NCAA athletics data

Softball programs, conferences, coaches, and records compiled from NCAA public statistics (via the sportsdataverse open-data project); division listings cross-referenced with NCSA College Recruiting's published D-I/D-II/D-III/NAIA college lists.

OpenStreetMap & CARTO

Base maps, geocoding (Nominatim), and driving routes (OSRM) for the map and road-trip features.

NACUBO Tuition Discounting Study

Context for our tuition guidance: average institutional discount rates at private colleges.

Spot an error in our data? Tell us — corrections make the App better for every family.
