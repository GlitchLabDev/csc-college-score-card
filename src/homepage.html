Questions

Is it really free?
Searching, the map, every school page and the cost data are free with no account. A $2.99/month subscription adds saved lists, comparison scoring, notes and visit planning.
Where does the data come from?
The US Department of Education — College Scorecard and IPEDS — plus the federal Equity in Athletics survey for sports. We don't invent rankings.
Will colleges start calling me?
No. We don't pass your details to schools or recruiters. That's the business model most college-search sites run on, and it isn't ours.
Does it cover trade schools and community colleges?
Yes — 1,925 certificate and trade schools and 1,251 community colleges, with the same federal data as the universities.
