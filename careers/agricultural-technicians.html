Common questions

How long does it take to become an agricultural technician?
Typically about two years for the degree, then a few months to a year of training on the job. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do agricultural technicians make?
The US median is $49,630 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree to become an agricultural technician?
Yes — a degree is the usual way in.
