How you get in

Most people are hired and trained on the job, so school is optional. A program can still help you stand out.

  1. High school diploma or equivalent (a program is optional for this job)
  2. Less than 5 years of experience in a related job
  3. Long-term on-the-job training

Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically no schooling after high school, then more than a year of training on the job, then less than 5 years of related work first. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do earth drillers, except oil and gas make?
The US median is $60,190 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
No. Most people are hired and trained on the job; a program is optional.
