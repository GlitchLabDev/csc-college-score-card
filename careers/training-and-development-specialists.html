Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically about four years for the degree, then less than 5 years of related work first. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do training and development specialists make?
The US median is $69,280 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
Yes, and employers also expect years of related experience first.
