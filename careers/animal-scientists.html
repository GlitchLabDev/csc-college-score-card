Common questions

How long does it take to become an animal scientist?
Typically about four years for the degree. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do animal scientists make?
The US median is $68,940 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree to become an animal scientist?
Yes — a degree is the usual way in.
Where can you train to become an animal scientist?
