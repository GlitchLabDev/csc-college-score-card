How you get in

Not an entry-level job: it usually takes a degree plus years of related work.

  2. Less than 5 years of experience in a related job

Common questions

How long does it take to become a funeral home manager?
Typically about two years for the degree, then less than 5 years of related work first. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do funeral home managers make?
The US median is $78,790 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree to become a funeral home manager?
Yes, and employers also expect years of related experience first.
