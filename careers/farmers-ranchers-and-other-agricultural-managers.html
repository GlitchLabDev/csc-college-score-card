How you get in

Most people are hired and trained on the job, so school is optional. A program can still help you stand out.

  1. High school diploma or equivalent (a program is optional for this job)
  2. 5 years or more of experience in a related job
  3. Work in: farmers, ranchers, and other agricultural managers

Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically no schooling after high school, then 5 years or more of related work first. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do farmers, ranchers, and other agricultural managers make?
The US median is $89,900 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
No. Most people are hired and trained on the job; a program is optional.
