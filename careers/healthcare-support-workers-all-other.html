Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically no schooling after high school. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do healthcare support workers, all other make?
The US median is $48,430 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
No. Most people are hired and trained on the job; a programme is optional.
Where can you train for this work?
