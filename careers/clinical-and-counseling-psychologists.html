Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically eight years or more of study, then an internship or residency, often one to three years. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do clinical and counseling psychologists make?
The US median is $100,580 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
Yes — a degree is the usual way in.
