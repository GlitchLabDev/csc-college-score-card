Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically no schooling after high school, then a few weeks of training on the job. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do word processors and typists make?
The US median is $49,280 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
No. Most people are hired and trained on the job; a program is optional.
