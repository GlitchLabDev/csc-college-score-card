How you get in

Not an entry-level job: it usually takes a degree plus years of related work.

  1. Bachelor's degree
  2. 5 years or more of experience in a related job
  3. Work as an emergency management director

Common questions

How long does it take to become an emergency management director?
Typically about four years for the degree, then 5 years or more of related work first. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do emergency management directors make?
The US median is $93,330 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree to become an emergency management director?
Yes, and employers also expect years of related experience first.
