Common questions

How long does it take to become a lighting technician?
Typically a few months to two years for the certificate, then a few weeks of training on the job. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do lighting technicians make?
The US median is $68,060 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree to become a lighting technician?
Not a full degree. A certificate or short program is the usual route.
