Common questions

How long does the training take?
Typically about two years for the degree. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do electro-mechanical and mechatronics technologists and technicians make?
The US median is $73,900 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree for this work?
Yes — a degree is the usual way in.
