Common questions

How long does it take to become a nurse practitioner?
Typically about six years of study in total. That is the usual route rather than a rule — people arrive by other paths all the time.
How much do nurse practitioners make?
The US median is $132,300 a year, meaning half earn more and half less. Pay varies widely by state, employer and experience.
Do you need a degree to become a nurse practitioner?
Yes — a degree is the usual way in.
Where can you train to become a nurse practitioner?
653 US colleges, community colleges and trade schools award credentials in a matching program. See which ones →
